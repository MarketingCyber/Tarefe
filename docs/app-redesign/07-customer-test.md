# Customer test of the prototype (phase 1)

Goal: find what confuses real customers before we build. 3 to 5 customers, about 20 minutes each, one at a time, on their own phone if possible.

## Setup
- Open the prototype (docs/app-redesign/prototype/index.html, or the web link once it exists) on the customer's phone.
- Mix: at least one customer over 50, one with little German, one who uses the old app often.
- Say: "We are testing the app, not you. Please think out loud. There are no wrong answers." Do not help unless they are stuck for more than a minute; then note where.
- Write down: where they hesitate, what they tap first, what they say. One sheet per customer.

## Tasks (read them out, one by one)
1. **Sign in.** "Sign in with your email." (Any 6-digit code works.)
2. **Find a deadline.** "Until when can you cancel your electricity contract?"
3. **Save money.** "Is there a cheaper electricity option? Tell your advisor you want it."
4. **Ask about one contract.** "You have a question about your dental insurance. Where do you write it?"
5. **Open a request.** "You are moving next month. Tell Tarefe about it for your electricity contract."
6. **Upload a document.** "Send Tarefe a photo of a letter you received."
7. **Referral.** "How would you invite a friend, and what do you get?"

## After the tasks (ask)
- What was the easiest part? What was the hardest?
- Did you understand that all Tarefe services are free? Where did you see it?
- Is anything missing that you use in the old app today?
- Persian, German or Russian: which language would you use?

## Scoring
For each task: done alone / done with a hint / not done, plus the time. A task that two or more customers could not do alone gets redesigned before phase 3 screens are finalised.
